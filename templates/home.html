Streamline your airline operations with our comprehensive flight management platform.

View Tables

Database Tables

Explore our database structure with these interconnected tables to manage flights, bookings, passengers, and more.

Flight

Manage flight information including source, destination, and times.

  • flightid (int4)
  • sourceairportid (int4)
  • destinationairportid (int4)
  • arrivaltime (time)
  • departuretime (time)
  • fair (numeric)

Booking

Track all passenger booking details and reservations.

  • bookingid (int4)
  • passengerid (int4)
  • scheduleid (int4)
  • seatno (text)
  • bookingdate (date)

Airport

Information about airports including location and contact details.

  • airportid (int4)
  • name (text)
  • city (text)
  • contactno (text)
  • address (text)

Plane

Details about aircraft including model, capacity and registration.

  • planeid (int4)
  • capacity (int4)
  • modeltype (text)
  • manufacturer (text)
  • licenseno (text)

Passenger

Passenger personal information and contact details.

  • passengerid (int4)
  • name (text)
  • address (text)
  • contactno (text)

Flight Schedule

Scheduled flights with dates and assigned aircraft.

  • scheduleid (int4)
  • flightid (int4)
  • planeid (int4)
  • date (date)

Employee

Airline staff information including roles and contact details.

  • employeeid (int4)
  • name (text)
  • designation (text)
  • address (text)
  • contactno (text)

Flight Employee

Association between flights and crew members.

  • flightid (int4)
  • employeeid (int4)
  • assignmentdate (date)
  • role (text)
